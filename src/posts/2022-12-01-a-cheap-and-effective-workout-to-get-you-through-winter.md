### Background

Recently I was doing the Spartan app for my workouts but decided to take a break. It’s not too pricey, ~90$ a month or ~$90 a year, basically the price of a gym membership for one or two months.

But I knew there was a workout I could do with my wife AND that really pushed me:

Jillian Michaels.

Biggest Loser coach, fitness woman extraordinaire. When my wife moved in with me (girlfriend at the time), we did another one of her DVDs and it always kicked my butt. Her videos are pretty brutal. Just hearing the word *Jillian* was enough to tighten my sphincter.

I’m up to the third level of the 30 day shred and it is rough. But I feel like I’m getting my money’s worth.

### Getting your money’s worth

I bought the video on [YouTube for 10 dollars](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TeE8b8n1Jtc). You can get it on Amazon for [5 dollars for a DVD](https://www.amazon.com/Jillian-Michaels-30-Day-Shred/dp/B00127RAJY) or [$6 on Prime Video](https://www.amazon.com/Jillian-Michaels-30-Day-Shred/dp/B00127RAJY). So what’s so good about it?

- It takes 20–30 minutes, including the cooldown
- You only need a [mat](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=yoga+mat&crid=RORQ3Y6CKXII&sprefix=yoga+mat%2Caps%2C146&ref=nb_sb_noss_1) (something in the $20 range should be fine), some [dumbbells](https://www.amazon.com/AmazonBasics-Pound-Neoprene-Dumbbells-Weights/dp/B01LR5RO5U/ref=sr_1_3?crid=ELSYUMLKSDDA&keywords=dumbbells+5+lbs&qid=1669875028&sprefix=dummbells+5+lb%2Caps%2C148&sr=8-3) (1.5lb — 10 lbs depending on how fit you are, I’d go for 3–5 to start) and the video
- It has strength, cardio and abs. So you won’t be leaving any of your body out of the fight

For $50 you can get a pretty good ~20 minute workout in. 125 BPM is a pretty good average for jumping around your living room.
