Something is not right. I’d signed a form I hadn’t read, because it promised a cash payout. $300 up front. And now I have to sit here and tell you how my week’s been, huh?

Whatever I snorted changed me.

*Ever since I was little I really couldn’t stand the sight of centipedes. The place my ma and I lived had them crawling all over. They loved the cold water pipes the HOA people said. At night I could see their legs and antenna twitching as they scurried around. My ma couldn’t give a s — t, knocked out on her ass, but they drove me nuts. Poisonous little f — kers.*

I got up the courage again to go see my guy again. This time I palmed the money extra tight, making sure I wouldn’t drop it. But as he started walking towards me I saw *them*. He didn’t even need to have his hand out, sure enough, the centipedes were following him. Their hairy thin bodies and piles of legs were all there.

*Where the f — k were all these centipedes coming from?* A part of me screamed, but the rest of me just turned and ran.

OK so my normal guy apparently had become the pied-centipede-piper. No big deal, I knew another person. My backup guy.

I found my way to my backup guy after a terrible night of sleep. Being sober sucks. He was on the other side of town so I had to take the bus. The weird thing about being on the bus was, it was super full.

Like normally I’d sit down right next to somebody, but all these wrapped up idiots were taking up all the seats. I had to stand for chrissakes.

Anyway I got to my backup guy, and sure enough I freak out again. This time I go into my pocket, and out crawls a freaking centipede right up into my sleeve. I can feel this f — er slithering all around insight my coat, so I go ballistic.

My backup guy is my backup for a reason, so he sees me drop the cash, picks it up and books it. By the time I realize what’s happened, I’ve thrown my coat and clothes off, and a good chunk of my money’s gone.

WHERE ARE THE F-ING CENTIPEDES COMING FROM?!

I mean I thought I was seeing things but I could *feel* it crawling on me. Sure enough when I took all my clothes off, didn’t see it anywhere. But they squish down real good and my clothes weren’t the cleanest.

All this to say I’ve been sober for like a week now. And I lost some of my snorting money. What the hell?! What the f — k did ya’ll do to me?

### CONFIDENTIAL: Guardian AI.ngel Phase 2 Clinical Trial Abstract

Augmented Reality efforts to drive behavior change have shown real progress in consenting customers [1], producing immediate positive behavior change. Through amygdala + hippocampus integration, we can generate different models of physical/visual/auditory sensations that the subject can experience. Models are run through various scenarios that the customer has planned to select the proper vector for behavior redirection.

Simple fear based misdirection can deter customers from highly dangerous behavior, resulting in temporarily heightened stress but positive redirection. [2] Also creating passive obstructions for the subject has been shown to produce nuanced behavior change in situations where the subject is not as highly motivated. [3]

Next phases of research will see if we can jumpstart motivation and produce novel steps towards ultimate desired behavior, moving beyond simply negating self destructive behaviors.
