### Who are These Books For?

Well… they were for me haha, so think man in his early 30s who’s interested in productivity books and biographies, but likes sci-fi/fantasy. I think they run the gamut but I’ll try to divide them all by category.

I’ve sorted each section by what I think was the most entertaining/informative at the top. If there’s interest in why I ordered them I can add more, but I figured a list would suffice, since you can try them out yourself.

### Productivity

- Leaders Eat Last — Simon Sinek
- The Goal — Eliyahu Goldrat
- Deep Work — Cal Newport
- Essentialism and Effortless — Greg McKeown
- $100M Offers — Alex Hormozi
- Who Moved My Cheese?/ Out of the Maze — Spencer Johnson

### Non Fiction/Humorous

- The Ultimate Hidden Truth of the World
- Kerplunk!
- Eve
- The Message
- The Body

### Biographies

- Greenlights (Matthew McConaughey)
- Born Standing Up (Steve Martin)
- The Race to Be Myself (Caster Semenya)
- I’m Glad My Mom Died (Jeannette McCurdy)
- Friends, Lovers, and the Big Terrible Thing (Matthew Perry)
- The Woman in Me (Britney Spears)

### Historical Fiction

- Shantharam
- All the Light We Cannot See

### Science Fiction

- Leviathan Falls (Book 9 of *The* *Expanse* Series)
- Foundation

### Fantasy/Misc. Fiction

- He Who Fights with Monsters (Books 1–11)
- The Bear
- The Big Door Prize
