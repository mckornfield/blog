> 13 Nathan replied, “The Lord has taken away your sin. You are not going to die. 14 But because by doing this you have shown utter contempt for[[a](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2%20Samuel%2012&version=NIV#fen-NIV-8301a "See footnote a")] the Lord, the son born to you will die.”

But not a great start.

Wanting s — t doesn’t make you worthy. It makes you covetous.

### So, Worthy How?

Being worthy means:

“Working *hard* to achieve an outcome that

(1) is merited by the effort you put in

(2) does not involve using or abusing anyone to achieve that outcome.”

That means if you just plink/plank/plunk on the guitar, but you’re not putting in real time to practice (hours! days! years!), don’t learn from others, and never getbetter, you shouldn’t be surprised that no one wants to listen to your music.

It also means that even if you are REALLY good at guitar, you shouldn’t have to force anybody to come listen to you play, but you have to keep striving to get your music out there and get your sound heard.

Also working hard doesn’t necessarily mean you have to slave your life away. It could even be *fun* for you, whatever it is.

But it does mean a time commitment to become excellent. And it means that you are a producer instead of being a consumer.

Well, the universe doesn’t owe you anything, in fact, one of the great Stephen Crane poems says it well:

> A man said to the universe:

> “However,” replied the universe,

### Wait Wasn’t this About Women?

It was but, it’s really not.

See women are not the end goal. Companionship is. And getting there requires being worthy.

Your end state as a man is not to be like King Solomon with 700 wives and 300 concubines. (Or is it? I guess it depends on [who you are](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elon_Musk)).

Nah, see King Solomon isn’t remembered because he had 2 wives for every day of the year: he’s remembered because *he was king and he was wise.*

He had power and he had skills.

Women aren’t your means to an end; they’re not something owed to you by God or the gods or whatever the f — k. Women are their own people. And they are attracted to all sorts of men, but let’s just line up two versions of yourself next to each other.

**One** is the leanest, most fit person in the world. He reads and has opinions that are informed instead of regurgitative. He has a good paying job, can afford a nice car and, maybe even a dang piece of property (in this economy?)

**The other** lives in his mom’s basement, loves pretending to know things he just saw on X, gamed until 3 AM last night, and just got let go from working at the grocery store.

Neither of those men had “great with the ladies” in their description. And maybe they both suck with women?

But I’ll tell you what, one of them is “ah, he just needs to go on a few dates,” and the other is “ick, no thank you.”

One is worthy… the other isn’t.

Be worthy!
