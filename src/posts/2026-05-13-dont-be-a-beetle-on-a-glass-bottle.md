My dad shared some wisdom withme when I was younger, and like a good teenage boy, I totally ignored him.

Part of it was I didn’t have a great relationship with him, and part of it I think is the idea that [you have to learn things on your own](https://happyfamilies.com.au/articles/why-einstein-said-we-cant-teach-our-children-anything).

Boys and men these days are even worse off than I was as a younger millenial, pulled away from the real world by **social media**, **porn** and **video games** into states where they don’t face the conditions that make them good at dating or set them up for any sort of success.

I know for sure I wasn’t.

I want to lay out why it’s worth attenuating the usage of all three and instead trading them for the embodied and much tougher act of trying to date a living, breathing woman.

I also want to try and flip around what probably doesn’t make sense to you as you experience it in the moment, but upon reflection you might realize: [easy choices, hard life; hard choices, easy life](https://www.reddit.com/r/quotes/comments/lkrswd/easy_choices_hard_life_hard_choices_easy_life/).

### Social Media is not social, but it is media

Social media is television. But the stars are you, your friends, and strangers you will never meet.

Social media is always curated, with faces edited with software, and only the most embarrassing, hilarious or cute moments end up on the platforms.

But life and dating are much more awkward, embarrassing, and ultimately boring than social media is.

This disparity feeds off systems that you evolved over millions of years to reward certain behaviors. It is the first form of a “**supernormal stimulus**” we will encounter on our dating misadventures.

There is a species of beetle that is [sexually attracted to brown shininess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julodimorpha_bakewelli).

If you offer them a glass beer bottle, they will attempt to have sex with it. It registers a 100 on their 1–10 attractiveness scale.

In the early days of social media, I could get the experience of looking at pretty, happy faces without putting out the effort to be near them, and it’s truly addicting. Over time I knew that I had to replace it with something, otherwise I’d spend more and more time with the bottle than with an actual human female.

**Instead try:**

Going to social functions that are not screen related. Go to church, or whatever faith you feel most comfortable with. Volunteer somewhere. Use Meetup, attempt to make embodied friends in a higher friction, lower dopamine setting. Being social means being around people, not ingesting them as content.

This will be more difficult for sure, but the rewards will be higher, as you’ll create real friendships and experiences instead of just consuming them. Lower lows, and higher highs.

### Porn is not sex

If you have never had sex but have only watched pornography, it really doesn’t work that way… at least it never has for me.

There’s this scene in the movie “[Zack and Miri Make a Porno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zack_and_Miri_Make_a_Porno)” where two of the characters are supposed to do a porn scene, but they just “have sex.” It’s funny in the context of the movie, because it’s a small intimate rocking motion, with sure, some of the sounds, but looks nothing like any of the other porn scenes in the movie.

If you compare that with pornography of any kind, that’s like a full on cage match, complete with unrealistic looking bodies (most likely you and your partner will not look like a porn star) doing much more aggressive, loud, overstimulating sorts of sexual contact.

It’s theater.

Back to the glass bottle, pornography ramps up the stimulus that appeals to males, but takes away two pretty essential elements:

- How difficult it is to actually be deserving of sex with a woman
- The intimacy of another person

Men who masturbate feel the same dopamine hit from it as they do from sex, but ultimately feel much lonelier. Dopamine is the “more” neurotransmitter (i.e., brain chemical), but there’s another, arguably more important one we leave on the table when we turn to porn away from actual women, and that’s [oxytocin](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6493805/). Basically it’s the connection/intimacy neurotransmitter.

The difference between observing (or arguably even participating in) pornographic sex is the lack of oxytocin. That and… you know, there being a woman there.

**Instead try:**

Dating an actual woman. This means more than just hitting up the apps, or asking women out (which can be tough on its own).

It means talking to women, being close enough to be their friends, and THEN getting the nerve to ask them out and get rejected.

Yes, you should seek the “no.”

As a boy/young manI was very afraid of rejection and so I kept my crushes as objects of affection that I dared not to ask. This created a situation where I could never lose (I never got rejected) but never won (I never got accepted).

Ultimately I think it’s a long term losing situation; if I’d asked these women out and gotten rejected, I would have realized I either needed to invest more time into myself (who ARE they attracted to?) or invest more time into seeking out other relationships (is there someone attracted to ME?)

I had plenty of women that had crushes on ME over time that I was oblivious to, because I had porn to keep me satiated and a crush to keep me paralyzed. Blegh.

Whatever you do, seek the “no” from women in the real world, and *say no* to the women in the virtual world. Because they are not women, they are shiny glass bottles.

### Video games are not achievement

I am not a sports guy. I played video games for a lot of my childhood and early adulthood.

But in adulthood I found myself growing distant from them, as I had a real relationship with a real woman (not a glass bottle!) and put more of my time and effort into improving my physical and financial situation.

I could write a [whole scree on video games](/posts/the-turning-inward-of-the-modern-man/) and their own form of dopamine hijack, but I think their ultimate problem is they take away the need to get out of the f — king house.

You can play online with friends, or play a single player game by yourself, and while there ARE ways to socialize playing online, they are not ways that will lead to a successful dating life ([unless you meet your wife playing Everquest](https://x.com/rolanberrypie/status/2033288925754937824)) .

Video games hijack our brains just like porn and social media, giving us rewards ([often variable rewards](https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/the-15-billion-question-have-loot-boxes-turned-video-gaming-into-gambling)) with relatively low friction (did you die? Revive! Or pay more!)

They create the sense of achievement while the true “achievers” are the companies making money off of selling you dopa-hits.

Part of dating is having something to discuss or do with the other person. I have met very few women that wanted to play (or talk about) video games with me (maybe I just can’t find these women?), which meant that I was spending a lot of time (hours and hours) growing a skill that:

- I could not use to date anyone
- Stole time away from any form of self improvement
- Was completely consumptive

You’ll notice the trend here with the things that make you suck at dating is that it’s *just consuming content*. Social media -> consume social experience. Porn -> consume sex. Video games -> consume virtual playgrounds.

Video games do involve some level of skill, and there are competitions etc. that I am obviously not qualified for, but I think 99% of people who play games will not lift themselves up to that level of rigor when it comes to games. They’re just using it as an escape.

Contrast this with something like learning an instrument, becoming good at making things, or playing a sport. These scenarios lead to a skill that you can demonstrate competence at, as well as make you more mentally or physically fit.

Unless you are placing in a smash tournament (and even then, 🤷), it’s unlikely a woman is going to be interested in you because of your gaming ability.

This is also to give the caveat that: games are not all bad, but they are not a drug that by taking more of it you will magically become happier and have better success dating.

In high school and college I played a ton of games, and for me it hurt my chances more than helped them when it came to dating.

In the very best case scenario, it did nothing to improve my chances (and as my wrestling coach said, “if you staying the same, and the other guy is getting better, you’ll lose”).

Dating is zero sum unfortunately, the pool of women is finite, there’s competition with other men, and your chances to meet and relate to them are finite.

I know from my wife that one of the greatest turn offs for her was one of her exes who had healthy servings of porn and video games while in their relationship. These represent ways that you go OUTSIDE a relationship to seek satisfaction, and I can see why she (and I’m sure plenty of other women) don’t like that.

### Instead try:

Increasing your odds by growing a skill that’s productive instead of consumptive. Make some money. Write. Learn an embodied skill, like an instrument or dancing. Play a sport, get in shape. You should be the envy of your past self, not just an older version with the same gaming habits.

These other things are ultimately games as well, but they’re ones that women find attractive.

Why do women like men with muscles? It shows you’re (hopefully) capable of protecting.

Men with money? You’re a provider.

Men with skills like making music and dancing? You have future potential or are fun to be around.

Women want a partner, not a kid. They’re already going to have to have a kid on their hands if they DO end up with someone and walk down that path, so they don’t need a second one. And these days they’ll just stay single, take care of the kid, and kick you to the curb.

### Summary

Dating is about turning down the things that women don’t give a s — t about and turning up the things that do, that means

- Stop liking women on a screen and start talking to them in real life
- Stop masturbating to women on a screen and start getting them to reject (or hopefully accept) you in real life
- Stop playing video games and start playing games that women are interested in seeing you win

If my past self knew this, maybe I’d be better off.

Hopefully some of this resonates with you.

Don’t be the beetle on the bottle, be the man.
