There were many things I was oneshot by as a kid (i.e., completely taken out of the running by them), and I think instead of trying to regulate or do some sort of “oh leave it up to the parents”, we just need to ban them.

Our kids (really our boys) are on a few different kinds of crack, and it’s turning them into pets and monsters.

This is not going to be a data backed rant, just a feelings backed one, mostly facing inward.

### Porn

As a kid, I was oneshot by porn, like I suspect many teenage boys are. Lots of usage, lots of shame, and very little interacting with females in the physical world.

And why should I? Interacting with real women was awkward, I was bad at it, and there was a machine I could just hook myself up to and get “the same result” (turns out it’s not the same, but you don’t know if you don’t know).

The upside to porn to me is so damn low. Yay, boys are placated by touching themselves while looking at screens? F\*\*k off.

We need to ban this. Not to the point of “oh in Louisiana porn is illegal.” I mean we treat it like crack. If you possess it, it’s a criminal offense. If you promulgate it, it’s a bigger criminal offense. If you create it, it’s a criminal offense. I don’t care about the porn industry, or porn sites. It’s not even that it’s immoral or that these women could be in the sex trade (which, are arguably *more* important reasons to ban it, see the next section).

It’s that we are unplugging men from the real world and plugging them into a fake one.

I get it, men have needs, and the women won’t always be there (especially when they are young and awkward). Guess that means you got to get to work to [make yourself worthy](/posts/be-worthy/) huh?

Or you can instead spend your time on —

### Video Games and Social Media

Ban them totally? Probably for the best.

But since we don’t allow kids to do anything else, we probably need to attenuate their usage instead. Maybe an hour each day? [A daily scroll?](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/rjzjay_s4DU)

Video games played with a friend, on the couch, are I think a relatively good thing. But games played for 10+ hours a week/day, at the exclusion of social interaction and physical movement? Replace the controller or mouse and keyboard with a needle and you get the picture.

World of Warcraft was the crack of my late elementary to high school years. The single best thing I did for myself was stop playing that game and games like it, which are full time jobs where *you pay:*

- you pay with your time
- you have to pay *per month*, and
- you pay in lost opportunities

Some people have fulfilling relationships and meet partners in these games. I think they’re the success stories. The other side is a generation of kids -> insulating themselves from the outside world and from the better versions of themselves.

Social media I think is quite bad on its own, but I don’t know that it has the same overpowering effect on men as it does on women. But we should severely limit it as well, as my interactions with social media were never healthy (if anything, they were highly comparative and *anti*social).

But I come back to this: if these things are really bad for us, why limit them to an hour or so a day? Smoke, or do crack, but only do it for an hour? Seems stupid.

We should just ban the damn things.

### Why do we ban things?

We ban them not just because they are bad for people (which all the things above I think are demonstrably bad, especially in excess). We ban them because, as my friend says, we have a moral case against them.

The moral case for me is simple: men who are addicted to porn and video games are lesser men. As a man who was addicted to those things *I* was a lesser man. I wouldn’t date the 18 or early 20s something version of myself.

My now wife but at the time girlfriend was essentially a slap in the face: “wake the f\*ck up, these things are not okay.”

They’re not okay. Let me say it again.

It’s not okay.

Let’s ban them.

I know in China they ban them; and kids will circumvent it, but I think we need to at least try. We need to treat these things as they are: a drag on our society and a real drag on our men. A corruption, a festering wound that is making men incapable of finding real companionship, and leaving women wondering “what the f — k do I even need you for?”

And enforcement?

What are you going to charge a kid with who doesn’t have any money?

Community service. Getting out and improving the world instead of letting it fall apart around them while they touch their controllers and weenies.

We also need to hold companies (pornhub, xvideos, game companies) just as accountable as the drug dealers they are. Just as we’ve essentially sanctioned the tobacco companies, they need to be sanctioned to.

In the case of porn sites though, I’d argue they just need to be nuked from orbit.

We have to hold kids (specifically boys) and young adults responsible, we can’t just think “oh hey it’s the parents fault” while they [work themselves to death and try to juggle parenthood](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt31806037/).

Ban that sh\*t. And give the enforcement some teeth.
