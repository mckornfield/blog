Imagine I call you and say I can offer you a managed fund, which grows with or beats the market, only for a 1% fee per year.

Or maybe I offer you tax lost harvesting or tax shelters like a life insurance policy.

If you’re a someone hoping to be wealthy, you might receive calls or messages like this, especially if you land a nice job.

Sure they might be trying to help you, but let’s think about it this way —

### If They Come to You, It’s To Help Themselves

People don’t call you out of the blue because they want to help you. They do it because they’re trying to *sell* to you. Wealth management is a massive industry, but it’s not rocket science. And wealth managers need *your wealth* to manage (and take fees from).

Here’s my two step program in place of wealth managers:

1. Read [*The Algebra of Wealth*](https://www.amazon.com/Algebra-Wealth-Formula-Financial-Security/dp/0593714024), [*I Will Teach You to Be Rich*](https://www.amazon.com/Will-Teach-You-Be-Rich/dp/0761147489)or [*The Simple Path To Wealth*](https://www.amazon.com/Simple-Path-Wealth-financial-independence/dp/1533667926)*.*
2. Talk with someone at Schwab, Fidelity Vanguard and they can walk you through basic investing

### Can’t Beat the Market

Warren Buffet had a famous bet that an index fund could beat any actively managed fund. (Index fund == a fund that tracks the stock market, read one of the above books!)

And [he won the bet](https://www.investopedia.com/articles/investing/030916/buffetts-bet-hedge-funds-year-eight-brka-brkb.asp).

There’s all sorts of reasons these funds aren’t better than just buying and holding the stock market (active trades triggering taxes, attempting to time the market, strange asset allocations), but the main reason is that these funds aren’t Bridgewater and they aren’t Berkshire (companies that have proven they can do as good or better than the market).

On top of the poor performance, these funds have something really bad going for them —

### 1%+ Fees Eat Away Returns

Imagine if I told you that our fees (i.e., how much we took out of the fund to run it for you) were only 1.5%, you’d think that wasn’t much right?

Now imagine that mean a million dollars over 30 years in fees, then it starts to sound like a lot, yeah?

![Source](/img/beware-the-wealth-vampires/0_jC-I0WWpY3OA-ecs.png.png)

*[Source](https://abundowealth.com/blog/how-fees-have-a-huge-impact-on-your-savings)*

Yeah… high fees suck. People that charge high fees are doing it so they can go to Morton’s and wear Armani suits while your retirement is 1/3 smaller.

If I had a cow, and you told me that you’d keep an eye on it, but it came back to me with 1/3 of its blood missing, you know what I’d call you?

*A blood sucker.*

### Summary

- People call you because it benefits them, not the other way around
- You can’t beat the market, and neither can these fund managers
- Their fees will suck out a huge chunk of your earnings

And you know how with vampires, they can’t enter your house if [you don’t let them in](https://www.buffyforums.net/forum/buffyverse/general-discussions/verse-mythology/739720-vampires-and-invitations)? Same goes for those phone calls.

Let them go to voicemail. Read some investing books. Buy your own funds.

Thanks for reading!
