I was inspired recently by a Philip Larkin poem recited on Ted Lasso (great poem, “[This Be the Verse](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/48419/this-be-the-verse)”.) Thought I’d let loose a poem that came to me while feeling pretty sore after a hike.

**Pain is a Teacher**

Pain is a teacher  
More constant than any preacher  
Not just Sunday but any day  
Pain will teach the lowest creature

Pain tells us when we’re very small  
What things we can and cannot do  
It tells us our needs, makes us bawl  
Ignore pain and he returns anew

But pain is a deaf teacher  
He knows not that we hear his lesson  
His presence a constant feature  
No matter the drugs we take to lessen

Despite our cries and wails and fear  
Pain offers us its deaf ear  
Its oration, the only thing it knows  
“Heed my call, or death follows”
