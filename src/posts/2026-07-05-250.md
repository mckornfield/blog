The lord’s fire burns against us

Not hell but sweltering heat

And so we’ll fight and fuss

About whose fault it must be

This is has happened once before

In a time Gilded and full of change

On the verdant breast of a new shore

Rich men came into power new and strange

But we know well what there’s to fix

As every day things break more and more

But the gerontocrats again have affixed

The lives they want from shore to shore

But revolution will come again

As we revolve and heat this sphere

For we’ll need to become denizens

Of a world born of a new fear

A land bought with native and black blood

With foreign sweat and native wonders

One that finds itself on the edge of a flood

After it has let itself cause many blunders

But as one last United Statesian breathes

So our nation will stand and fight

Because no matter what the oligarchs bereave

They will never snuff out liberty’s light

Rapists and monsters, old fucking pigs

Who kiss the girls and make them cry

Who chain us to gas and oil rigs

Who send us emails with which we’ll comply

They will pass, all of them will pass

And so our nation will pass too

But the heart that fights them will everlast

The cracked ring of freedom will ring true

So starting this seventh month, this fourth day

Of this two hundred, fifty year rebellion

We'll show our monsters, make them pay

Exorcise, and send them to hell again
