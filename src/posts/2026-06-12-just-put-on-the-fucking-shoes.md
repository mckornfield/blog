### Running sucks

As a pre teen and teenager, I was a terrible runner. Because I didn’t play sports or try to do it. I spent time wondering how kids got to be so fast, and of course I knew they ran more than I did, but how? Running hurts. Running sucks. I don’t want to!

The answer: put on the fucking shoes

In college I’d run a few miles HARD and be absolutely gassed. No more running! I’m DONE! Go back to the elliptical or weights, running sucks too bad.

Then after college I told myself I’d do a spartan race with some family. I had to train so I wouldn’t run out of gas, while climbing, carrying and pulling a bunch of shit.

How did I get into decent running shape for once? What was my strategy? A big plan with x miles a day at y pace?

No. I put on the fucking shoes.

### Starting is the hard part

Running is in your head. It always sucks to run, even if you’re in good running shape (trust me, I just did a marathon and it still sucked, even with all the miles I’d put in).

On sundays I’d do my long runs. 8 miles, 9, going all the way to 20 miles then back down to 17 before the race. My feet felt like I’d been running across lava.

But every Sunday, the single hardest part about running wasn’t the miles themselves (well hell I ran all the way here, I might as well run back). The hardest part was *starting*. And the only answer to my desire to wimp out?

Put on the Fucking Shoes (tm).

Life is not about avoiding pain, it’s about crossing that crummy threshold where you know pain is coming, but you step into it anyway. It could even be death or maiming, but what makes humans great is the ability to know suffering is ahead, and voluntarily push into it.

Are you looking to lose weight? Looking to run for more than a mile without dying?

Don’t start some crazy 5k plan.

Don’t join a running club then walk the thing.

Don’t watch 50 youtube videos on running form.

Go to a store. Buy a pair of shoes.

Then put on the fucking shoes.

And while you’re wearing them, well, you might as well sweat a bit.

You aren’t a runner, or a fat person, you’re someone who puts on the shoes, or someone who sits on your ass.

I know which version of myself I prefer.

Thanks for reading, and remember…
