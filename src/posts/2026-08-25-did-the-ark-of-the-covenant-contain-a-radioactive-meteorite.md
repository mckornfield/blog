### Viewing Religious Teaching as a Spectrum

There is I think a spectrum, where on one end you have the literal word of God, as it happened, no questions asked, and on the other end, you have mythology, clearly inspired by other tales, not based on actual events.

My belief is the Bible and any religious text ranges from one end of the spectrum to the other, but somewhere in the middle lies what I believe these passages of the Ark are about:

*An explanation of phenomena that was (is?) beyond human understanding*

### Down with the Sickness

When I read these exacting measurements, I think of… a manual or engineering textbook. Something where the exact dimensions *do* matter.

### “Jewish Physics”

In the late 19th and early 20th century, nuclear physics transformed our understanding of the physical world.

A lot of these early experiments were [death sentences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Curie#Death), knowing what we do now about radiation sickness, and trying to figure out all the different ways to get elements to radiate.

The Nazis would coin a term “German Physics ([Deutsche Physik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Physik))” to counter the “Jewish Physics” of Albert Einstein.

Among Einstein were others like Bohr, Rutherford, and a slew of scientists who started to examine the structure of the atom. Some were Jewish, others were not, but to tie back to our Ark, we should note one experiment.

In it, Rutherford took *gold* (yes, like the same stuff that the Ark was coated in), and proved that alpha particles (a type of radiation) would [deflect at odd angles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Rutherford#Model_of_the_atom) when launched at the foil, showing the structure of atoms and a form of “radiation shielding,” albeit weak, with the thin gold foil.

Gold is a heavy metal. Its atomic number (basically the number of protons in the center) is 80.

![Gold is heavy, atomically heavy](/img/did-the-ark-of-the-covenant-contain-a-radioactive-meteorite/0_-a2loSnBxyyvKH_2.png.png)

*Gold is heavy, *atomically heavy**

One of gold’s ability’s, because it is so atomically dense, is that it can deflect radiation, unlike lighter elements which might do little/nothing to defend against radiation.

So let me paint a picture of what might have “happened,” based on some hand wavey ideas about radiation, and why the Ark and its contents have the properties they do, and why the boring dimensions *probably matter*.

### Once Upon a Time on Mount Sinai

Moses came up the mountain, and God gave Moses the tablets. What do we think that *actually* looked like to Moses?

God is above the Earth, “in heaven,” and so God lobbed a meteorite onto Mount Sinai. A bright light flashing in the sky, and made an enormous crash as it collided with the ground.

The meteorite didn’t have to be very big (if it were… there wouldn’t be any more Mt. Sinai), having burned up a good amount in the atmosphere, so only a small, smoldering chunk was left, and a very explosive, scary sight for anyone who was around to see (i.e., Moses).

The rock itself might have had “[trace amounts](https://sites.wustl.edu/meteoritesite/items/radioactive/)” of radioactive material, or it might have collided with rock that itself contained radioactive material and by chance, unearthed it. Even small amounts, when struck just right, can release a lethal dosage of radiation.

[Moses face would have been lit up by this hot, radioactive rock.](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus%2034%3A29-35&version=NIV)

Exodus 34 goes:

> 29 When Moses came down from Mount Sinai with the two tablets of the covenant law in his hands, he was not aware that his face was radiant because he had spoken with the Lord.

The Bible says that Moses *himself* chiseled the words out, so they came from God in *some* form, but not in the way they were written. Maybe they (literally) fell from heaven.

Over time people would get sick around the tablets, especially if they were jostled/ knocked in a way that would start [a fission reaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_fission), producing a lethal dosage to whoever happened to do so (sorry Uzzah).

Their sacredness would come with a level of protection, both to preserve the tablets (who were fissiling away to nothing) and to protect the caretakers of the tablets, the priests and holy men who had to carry this radioactive material around.

Otherwise they would be struck dead or afflicted with tumors.

This required *precise* instructions on how to move the Ark (always with the poles), who could touch it, what was to be worn while touching it, and getting back to where we started, *excruciating detail* on how to construct all the things that contained it.
