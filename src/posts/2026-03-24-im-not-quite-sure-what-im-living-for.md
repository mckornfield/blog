First off: don’t worry this isn’t a suicide note. Just some ruminations.

### A School Full of Children

A few days/ maybe a week or so ago at this point, we blew up a school full of kids in Iran. Maybe it was a mistake, bad intelligence, I don’t really know. During my entire life I’ve seen school/child related violence increase, at least in media coverage, but I believe the statistics back it up.

![Required graph that supports my views ( source )](/img/im-not-quite-sure-what-im-living-for/0_vzaXeBk_YVVEreK3.png.png)

*Required graph that supports my views ([source](https://www.reddit.com/r/dataisbeautiful/comments/84obcd/school_shootings_per_year_since_1950_moving/))*

I’ve heard politicians opine about how we just need more gun control, or how we need to track criminals/mental health issues better, but I’ve just seen none of that happen. I’ve just seen more kids get killed.

And it just makes me think…

What am I living for? To support systems that directly lead to dead children? (either via missiles or crazy person with an AR-15?)

I’m not even talking about Israel/Gaza or any of the other madness of the last few years.I just mean kids going to school, and getting blown away.

I guess I’m supporting those systems? Including the ones where [pedophiles get to do whatever](https://www.justice.gov/epstein) they want with these kids? We need to fill a building up with them and blow *that* up. Anyway… onto another fun topic —

### Suicide

A guy I knew in high school killed himself. He was a great dude, very funny and way smarter than I am. But in college he ended it. I didn’t attend the funeral; I wouldn’t say I was a good friend, probably at best an acquaintance.

But my whole life I’ve struggled with these sorts of thoughts too. My self worth is probably better characterized as “self unworth.” There are days where, I fucking hate myself. So much it hurts.

Maybe that’s what happened to the guy from high school? Or maybe he had some other reasons; he’s his own person so I don’t fucking know.

That’s not to say you need to call the suicide hotline, I have lived long enough to develop some coping strategies. Generally they end up being running a lot, writing screes like this, and throwing away a lot of shit.

And a lot of times it’s just food; I am a hangry person for sure.

But boy oh boy (literally) are [men/young boys killing themselves](https://aibm.org/research/data-spotlight-suicide-deaths-among-young-men/). And I can’t help but empathize with them.

But fear not suicidal boys, if you make lots of money, you can give it all away at gunpoint!

### Taxes and Cost of Living

I made more money last year than I had any year before (yay me). I paid more taxes too. So much of “tax strategies” are just mechanisms to avoid paying them. But aren’t we supposed to? I guess not if you’re super rich or old (or both!)

I moved to California, and it’s a state that is notorious in its tax rate in the US. Not surprising that when I moved here from WA, I paid a lot more in tax.

![](/img/im-not-quite-sure-what-im-living-for/0_29dhVyNWIUvWOkIl.png.png)

What was surprising to me is that… I really just don’t know where the money ends up. The elementary school near me apparently gets a shortfall of 20% under the funding it asks for, and a lot of the things that are good about the school are the result of a well funded PTA (AKA, people paying even more out of their pocket beyond taxes).

There’s tons of homeless and trash all over downtown San Diego, to the point where, if I didn’t know any better, I’d think I was living in the slums.

![](/img/im-not-quite-sure-what-im-living-for/0_vt5TTHaXCICmWOtF.jpg)

And the electricity costs here are the [highest in the nation](https://www.reddit.com/r/sandiego/comments/1oiu5fw/electric_cost_in_the_united_states/). Housing costs are disgusting, between CEQA, blocking development and Boomers refusing to move out of their single family houses, [CA is missing 3–4 million housing units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_housing_shortage). And because of Prop-13, all these older people who made building in CA impossible don’t even bear the [tax on their properties](https://www.nber.org/digest/apr05/lock-effect-californias-proposition-13?page=1&perPage=50). Fuckers.

I make pretty good money, but the fact that about 50% of it will get peeled away to make sure there’s trash on the street and boomers living in houses makes me think: *I’m Not Quite Sure What I’m Living For.*

I see the results of where the money goes (blowing up kids in the middle east, [substantially subsidizing old people’s existence](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/spending-on-children-and-the-elderly/)), and the results fucking suck.

### Having Kids

I have one daughter. It’ll be my only kid for sure. She has all sorts of stuff she’s dealt with in her four short years, in terms of lots of water in her ears (surgery for that, $8000 after insurance) and now has apraxia, meaning she speaks like a 2–3 year old, as a 4–5 year old (and that I pay for speech therapy, 3 times a week, $130 each time, until I meet the deductible).

On top of that, she’s having all sorts of behavioral issues at school, probably stemming from her speech problem.

It sucks. I have a non-verbal brother who is functionally disabled and lives with my mom, and I had lots of ear problems as a kid, so these are most likely my bad genes. I see lots of younger people who, instead of having kids, have dogs or nothing, and I don’t blame them. (Hopefully they have better genes than I do haha).

I love my daughter, but dealing with the problems she has encountered is a pain I didn’t realize I was singing up for. I basically get to feel shitty about myself AND someone else (my bad genes get to live on!). Woo parenting.

### Tang Ping 躺平 — Lying Flat

Instead of killing yourself, there’s a custom in China where you just “check out.” Living a low desire life. I think in an alternate timeline, I could see myself putting in the bare minimum at a job, then heading home to play as many hours of video games as possible before waking up to do it all over again.

But recently I wanted to play a game, and I just didn’t even care enough to open it up. What’s going to be the result, a bunch of dopamine hits with frustration pumped in between? Throwing dollars at Gabe Newell so I have something to do? “Lying flat” seems like a worse waste of time than anything.

Games have lost most of their appeal to me. I just don’t see it.

### What Gives Me Any Hope

I’ve been reading the Bible recently (gasp). And the stories in there are basically the same things we see playing out (Samson brings the pillars down on the *philistines* i.e., the Palestinians, there’s lots of stoning and war/genocide). It turns out that the stories from 5000 years ago are still relevant, because we’re still acting like it’s 5000 years ago.

What gives me hope for the future, and what keeps me going, is that I get a *lot* out of new experiences. All the pain and dumb shit that I’ve experienced or done is part of a cycle that an ancient text echoes pretty well. I’m sure the Upanishads or the Dharma are good echoes of the things I’m experiencing as well.

I saw some people get baptized recently and it spoke to me; not in a “I need to baptized right now!” sort of way, just in a “powerful experience” sort of way. I personally don’t think I’m worth of such an experience, but just being there to witness it meant a lot to me.

I’m living for these experiences, and yes, some of them really suck.

- I have nothing good to say about blowing up/shooting kids. Or raping them. We should fucking stop doing that, and hold people accountable, regardless of their bank account size.
- The kid who I went to school with who killed himself, I got to bear witness to his life. He was an amazing person.
- Maybe some day I will be the incumbent complaining about tax policy, but I doubt it. We should fucking stop paying so old people can live well while young people are fucking struggling. Maybe my experience will be trying to fight this stupid battle.
- I got to see my daughter’s birth. Since then I’ve seen many amazing things, and she’s made me feel all the ups the roller coaster along with the downs. She’s amazing.
- I have a lot of fun playing games with my daughter too :)

Thanks for reading! Go outside and touch grass. I know I fucking need it.
