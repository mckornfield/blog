Happy halloween everyone! Thought I’d share a set of scary events that happened in a basement where I grew up. Boo!

### The house on the hill

In elementary school I lived on a hill in New Rochelle, NY. The house we lived in had three levels, staggered a bit, and as the title suggests, a basement.

This basement had dark grey columns, our water heater, and a washer and dryer, along with a bunch of random things in the corners. It was one stairwell straight down, a tiny window to the backyard, and one long room with no divisions beyond columns and couches. The shadows could appear very strange over the columns, almost as though something or someone had moved.

And it was probably haunted.

### My aunt’s encounter

My aunt and uncle came to live with us for a time, along with their kids, my cousins. While they were there, my aunt was down doing laundry, facing away from most of the room.

She felt a tap on her shoulder, as though her husband had snuck up on her. She turned and saw only the shadows, to which she dropped her laundry and bolted upstairs.

### My encounters

When I went in the basement it was mostly during the day, but even then strange things happened.

I remember seeing movement by the far corner of the basement that I couldn’t explain, like an extra shadow that flitted out or seemed to move as though another entity were down there. The fear I started to feel would turn into dread, if I had to go there in the night.

One night I went down and turned the unfortunately fluorescent light on, and checked the far corner as I often did. I can’t remember why I went down there, but as I turned my head from the corner, a swelling “aaaaaaaAHHHHH!” Seemed to emanate from both the corner and inside my head at the same time.

So I bolted upstairs, just like my aunt.

### Why I believe it was really haunted: our dog

We had a stocky black lab called Nicky who had the misfortune of being put in the basement while people were cleaning the house. He scratched and whined as dogs do, but we left the house and came back to something I hadn’t seen before.

When we re-entered the house, Nicky was walking around happily, ignoring the cleaners.

On inspection of the basement door, the bottom section had been completely knocked out, leaving a Nicky sized hole at the bottom. Granted, it was a cheap wooden door, but what could have motivated Nicky to do that… what indeed?

### Ideas for why

The basement had very strange lighting, from the one tiny window and the columns spread throughout. It was an older house as well, that might have had plumbing that [emitted infrasound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrasound). And my aunt was pretty superstitious, while I was afraid of the dark.

My dog though? He just wanted to hang out with the cleaners? Or maybe he was trying to escape something.
