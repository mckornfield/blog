A letter to any men who aren’t working or doing their fair share

### Dirty Unemployment

Mike Rowe made the statement in multiple places that around [7 million able bodied men aren’t working](https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/careers/mike-rowe-warns-the-real-crisis-isn-t-skills-or-ai-but-the-will-to-work-as-more-able-bodied-men-exit-the-workforce-what-does-america-lose/ar-AA1RqxDe), while simultaneous there is a massive shortage in the trades (to the tune of 5 people retiring for every 2 that are joining).

Our unemployment numbers in this country are warped, just like every other metric that politicians use to maintain a semblance that “things are alright.” Well they’re not fucking alright.

I know too many young men (I’m related to some of them!) who are not only jobless, but hopeless screen zombies left by their parents to turn into house pets.

And while gig jobs proliferate, they do not provide benefits, stability, or even decent wages, leaving employees with no economic security while staying paycheck to paycheck scenario.

### The Immigrants “Took Our Jerbs”, Because We Aren’t Doing Them

If you haven’t seen the South Park episode [where time traveling immigrants come and take everyones “jerbs”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=APo2p4-WXsc), then you might not know why I keep spelling “jobs” wrong.

Mike Rowe has pointed out that for every five people that retire from the trades, two people join. If we deported all legal and illegal immigrants, I’d doubt we’d have any tradespeople left.

When I lived outside Portland, in Vancouver WA, every single person who did work on my place was Ukrainian or an older guy. There is a religious refugee population of Ukrainians in the area, and they are willing to do (and do well) all the blue collar, GC jobs that I didn’t see any young people of any other race or background do.

When I lived in CT or VA, same story, old guys as GCs and everyone that worked for them was a hispanic immigrant. I worked for a couple of these guys in high school, and the work was definitely hard. But my alternatives? School school school and hope for a good white collar job.

### The Grift of the Grad

One of the greatest grifts of the last 30+ years has been higher education backed by federal student loans that are never forgiven.

I know plenty of people who, years and years on, are still paying their loans, because the degree (or degrees) they borrowed for did not produce the income to pay the loans off.

Too much of our decision making, especially as young adults, is done based on “what we want to do”, but immigrants know better, i.e, “what other people need you to do” is much more important. And this goes for blue collar as well as white collar immigrants (i.e., H1Bs)

I got a degree in physics because the school I went to didn’t offer an engineering degree, but the most important work that I did while at that school was connect myself to smart, capable people who helped me find a job.

A degree without those connections is trash. And many of these connectionless degrees exist, costing more and more each year to people who are having a tougher time finding stable employment with useless degrees.

### The Other People who Really Did Take Our Jobs

[China china china china](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RDrfE9I8_hs).

Trump isn’t wrong to express ire for China taking away the jobs, but he’s a bit late.

![Source](/img/just-get-to-work/1_SzVhR9dfYOJW--dGeBugfg.png.png)

*[Source](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/MANEMP)*

There isn’t any amount of tariffs that can fix the fact that America has hollowed out its industrial sector, and pushed its citizenry to service based work that, while being “productive”, does not create the goods we consume.

I’m writing this on a Macbook (made in China), if I flip over every object in my line of sight, I’ll find a label that reminds me: we don’t make goods in the USA. Other than oil and agricultural products, the US is a major importer of stuff. And tariffs won’t singlehandedly rebuild our missing industrial center.

And our reason for shipping these jobs overseas? (or over borders?)

The American worker is too costly, with healthcare, pensions, unionization, companies can instead get 5–10x the labor for the same cost, a container ship or land border away.

That being said, there is still a huge demand for domestic labor. As said above, the trades have a massive labor deficit, and while our manufacturing sector has (stupidly) shrunk, we still need men who are willing to turn screws and build sh\*t.

### Women Don’t Need Men Who Won’t Do the Work

“The Work” is a massive category. Historically that was doing something that got you money or some form of currency (or, at the very least, stopped your family from being killed). But recently as women are being deployed into the workforce, men need to be deployed into the childcare front.

This weekend I volunteered at my church to watch the K-2 cohort. I watched my own kid along with 6 others during the service. It was work. To say it’s “Women’s work” is to show how much of an idiot you are. Kids need adult men to care about them as much as their mothers.

Millennial dads are showing up, but I know many men who are single or have no intention of having kids. It makes me sad, because while it is a lot of work, it’s also “The Work”, raising humans is what all these things we do are for: why we fall in love, why we have sex, why we try to earn more and be better versions of ourselves.

It’s to make it possible to have our own kids, and for them to be successful, financially, physically and spiritually.

### You Are Not Too Good for Anything

As a man who is unemployed, you don’t have the ability to say you’re too good to do a certain type of work, or because the system is set up against you.

The amount of work that needs to be done just to keep things from falling apart is staggering. Millions of homes, bridges, structures and institutions are barely holding on, as our labor force ages and can’t take care of the hollow shell that once was a great country.

We have to remember that doing nothing is the same as making things worse; women know very well that if you do nothing while a child is crying, they can get sick or die.

Our country is in dire need of men who will do work inside and outside of the house, and work where we get our hands dirty (either with dirt or wiping poopy butts).

We’re not too good for any of it. So let’s get off our butts, stop playing with games, gambling and drugs, and do the work.
